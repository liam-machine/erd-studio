Accessibility Considerations
Cool/Warm: Best overall accessibility with distinct hue separation (blue-teal-orange).

CI/CD Status: Green for approved follows DevOps conventions. Good for teams familiar with pipeline status colours.

Traffic Light: Universal recognition but may be problematic for red-green colour blindness. The app uses text labels alongside colours to maintain accessibility.
